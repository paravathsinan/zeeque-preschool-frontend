# About Zeeque Preschool

## Overview

ZeeQue Preschool is a unique and state-of-the-art Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE) program specially designed for children between 3 to 6 years of age. It is Kerala's fastest growing Islamic Montessori preschool network, and has been operating since 2013.

ZeeQue Preschool is more than just a school — it's a comprehensive ecosystem designed to nurture children, empower parents, and train world-class educators for a brighter future.

## Network Scale (as of 2026)

- **4 Countries** — Operating in multiple countries
- **152 Schools** — Branches across Kerala and beyond
- **15,000+ Happy Students** — Children who have gone through the program
- **2,200+ Trained Teachers** — Educators trained through our programs

## Age Groups and Stages

ZeeQue Preschool spans three progressive developmental stages:

| Stage | Name                        | Age Range |
|-------|-----------------------------|-----------|
| LZQ   | Lower Zeeque Preschool      | 3–4 years |
| MZQ   | Middle Zeeque Preschool     | 4–5 years |
| UZQ   | Upper Zeeque Preschool      | 5–6 years |

Each stage is thoughtfully designed to match the child's developmental needs.

## Educational Philosophy

ZeeQue draws upon a diverse range of educational approaches, methods, and techniques — all geared towards nurturing:
- Creativity
- Collaboration
- Joyful learning
- Safety in an Islamic-values-based environment

At ZeeQue, children learn and acquire life skills through play at their own pace. There is no formal teaching pressure — sessions are fun-filled, creative, and engaging with a focus on personality development and overall well-being.

## Theme-Based, Play Way Method

- Children learn through structured and free play
- Weekly theme-based curriculum modules bring new worlds to explore (animals, space, seasons, professions)
- No textbooks, no pressure, no rote memorization
- Each child progresses at their own pace
- Every classroom activity balances learning, play, and rest

## Our Core Values

1. **Islamic Values** — Shaping children's lives in accordance with Islamic values through child-friendly mentoring and guidance.
2. **Creativity** — Encouraging imagination and creative expression through art, play, and hands-on exploration every single day.
3. **Collaboration** — Building teamwork and social skills through group activities, shared projects, and collaborative learning.
4. **Joyful Learning** — Making education fun and engaging so children develop a lifelong love for learning and discovery.
5. **Safety First** — Supervised single-door entry/exit, secure fenced play areas, and trained staff ensuring complete child safety.
6. **Holistic Growth** — Nurturing cognitive, emotional, physical, and social development through a well-rounded ECCE curriculum.

ZeeQue centers are designed to be healthy, safe, and stimulating:

- **Child-Friendly Classrooms** — Spacious, colorful rooms with age-appropriate learning materials
- **Sand Pit** — Sensory play area for creative exploration and tactile development
