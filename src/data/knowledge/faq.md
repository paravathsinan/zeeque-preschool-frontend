## Programs and Age Groups

**Q: What programs does ZeeQue offer?**
A: ZeeQue offers the following programs:
1. **3-Year Kindergarten (LZQ, MZQ, UZQ)** — for children aged 3–6 years (our flagship program)
2. **Grade Stream (I–IV)** — English Medium Primary Madrasa for Grades 1 to 4
3. **Diploma in ECCE** — 6-month teacher training program
4. **TTGS** — Residential teacher training for Grade Stream educators
5. **PEP** — Parent Empowerment Programme (ongoing)

**Q: What is the age requirement for admission?**
A: Children aged 2 to 6 years are accepted:
- LZQ (Lower Zeeque): Age 3–4 years
- MZQ (Middle Zeeque): Age 4–5 years
- UZQ (Upper Zeeque): Age 5–6 years
- Grade Stream starts from age 6 (Standard I)

**Q: What is the difference between LZQ, MZQ, and UZQ?**
A: These are the three progressive stages of ZeeQue's 3-Year Kindergarten:
- **LZQ (Lower):** Age 3–4, introductory sensory learning, first Quran exposure
- **MZQ (Middle):** Age 4–5, reading readiness, numbers, Quran memorization
- **UZQ (Upper):** Age 5–6, advanced literacy, leadership, completes 1 Juz of Quran

**Q: What is the Nursery equivalent at ZeeQue?**
A: The MZQ stage (Middle Zeeque Preschool, age 3–4 years) is equivalent to a standard Nursery program.

**Q: What is the Grade Stream program?**
A: Grade Stream is ZeeQue Preschool English Medium Primary Madrasa — an extension of the ZeeQue philosophy into formal primary education, covering Grades I through IV.

## Curriculum and Teaching Approach

**Q: What teaching method does ZeeQue use?**
A: ZeeQue uses a theme-based, play way method. Children learn through structured and free play with no formal textbook-based teaching. Each week introduces a new theme (animals, space, seasons, professions) and children learn at their own pace in a stress-free, joyful environment.

**Q: Is ZeeQue an Islamic school?**
A: Yes, ZeeQue integrates Islamic values throughout its curriculum. Children learn to recite and memorize the Quran (1 Juz in 3 years), practice daily Adhkar (supplications), learn Islamic values and character, and celebrate Islamic festivals. Spiritual education is fused with modern academic learning.

**Q: Do children learn multiple languages at ZeeQue?**
A: Yes. ZeeQue provides trilingual education:
- **Arabic** — reading, writing, and speaking
- **English** — communication and literacy
- **Malayalam** — mother tongue fluency

Children are trained to communicate, read, and write in all three languages.

**Q: How much Quran do children memorize at ZeeQue?**
A: Over the 3-year kindergarten program, children are trained to recite the Holy Quran and memorize at least **1 Juz' (one chapter) within 3 years**. The UZQ stage includes Juz' completion.

**Q: What is the teacher-to-child ratio at ZeeQue?**
A: ZeeQue has **3 dedicated mentors per classroom**, ensuring every child receives personalized attention and care.
