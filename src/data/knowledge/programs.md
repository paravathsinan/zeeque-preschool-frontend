## 1. Three-Year Kindergarten (3 Year KG) — Flagship Program

The flagship program is a three-year Kindergarten package for children aged 3 to 6. Children progress through three stages: LZQ, MZQ, and UZQ.

### Key Features of the 3 Year KG

- Play-based, stress-free learning environment
- Theme-based weekly curriculum modules
- Trilingual education — Arabic, English, Malayalam
- Quran recitation and Islamic values integration
- 3 mentors per classroom for personalized care
- Sensory play with sand pit, splash pool, and ponds
- Research-backed curriculum
- Individual progress tracking

### LZQ — Lower Zeeque Preschool (Year 1)

- **Age:** 3–4 years
- **Focus:** Introduction to the world of learning
- Introduction through sensory exploration, basic social skills, and gentle guided play
- Children discover letters, colors, shapes, and begin their Quran journey
- **Key Skills:**
  - Sensory exploration
  - Basic motor skills
  - Color and shape recognition
  - Social interaction basics
  - First Quran exposure

### MZQ — Middle Zeeque Preschool (Year 2)

- **Age:** 4–5 years
- **Focus:** Building on foundations with structured theme-based activities
- Children develop reading readiness, numerical concepts, creative expression, and deeper Islamic values understanding
- **Key Skills:**
  - Reading readiness
  - Number concepts
  - Creative arts
  - Teamwork and sharing
  - Quran memorization

### UZQ — Upper Zeeque Preschool (Year 3)

- **Age:** 5–6 years
- **Focus:** Preparing for the transition to formal schooling
- Advanced literacy, critical thinking, leadership skills, and complete Juz' memorization
- **Key Skills:**
  - Advanced literacy
  - Critical thinking
  - Leadership skills
  - School readiness
  - Juz' completion (1 complete Juz of the Quran memorized in 3 years)

## 2. Grade Stream (I to IV) — Primary Education

**ZeeQue Preschool English Medium Primary Madrasa** extends the ZeeQue philosophy into formal primary education.

- Covers **Grades I through IV** (Standard 1 to 4)
- Continues the seamless blend of academic excellence and Islamic values education
- Students who graduate from the KG program transition naturally into the Grade Stream
- Ensures continuity in pedagogy, values integration, and personalized mentoring

## 3. Diploma in ECCE — For Teachers

A comprehensive **six-month pre-school teacher training programme** that equips aspiring educators with everything they need to deliver world-class early childhood education.

| Detail    | Information                              |
|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| Duration  | 6 Months (also available as 35-Day Residential) |
| Focus     | Pre-school teacher preparation           |
| Method    | Classroom + hands-on practical           |
| Outcome   | Certified ECCE (Early Childhood Care & Education) educator |

### What Diploma ECCE Covers

- Modern ECCE pedagogy and teaching methods
- Child development science
- Classroom management for early years
- Multilingual and Islamic-integrated curriculum delivery
- Practical hands-on teaching sessions

## 5. PEP — Parent Empowerment Programme

A unique initiative that keeps parents informed and involved in their child's educational journey.

- **Nutrition** — Dietary guidance for optimal brain development and physical growth
- **Parenting Skills** — Effective parenting strategies, communication, and positive reinforcement
