# ZeeQue Preschool Admissions

## Admissions for 2026

ZeeQue Preschool admissions for the 2026 academic year are currently open!

**Online Admission Portal:** https://admission.zeeque.in/

At ZeeQue, we believe in making the admission process simple, transparent, and parent-friendly. Our goal is to ensure a smooth start to your child's educational journey in a nurturing and value-based learning environment.

---

## Age Eligibility

| Program / Stage             | Age Requirement |
|-----------------------------|-----------------|
| LZQ (Lower Zeeque)          | 2–3 years       |
| MZQ (Middle Zeeque)         | 3–4 years (Nursery equivalent) |
| UZQ (Upper Zeeque)          | 4–5 years       |
| 3-Year KG (full program)    | 3–6 years       |
| Grade Stream (Primary)      | 5+ years (Std I) |

Children aged 2 to 6 years are accepted across the preschool programs.

## How to Apply

1. **Online:** Apply at **https://admission.zeeque.in/**
2. **In Person:** Visit our campus at Zahra Park, Koduvally, Kozhikode
3. **Phone:** Call our admissions counsellor at **+91 9072 500 435**
4. **Email:** Send enquiries to **contact@zeeque.in**

You can also download the admission form from our website (available via the top navigation bar).

| Document                   | Details                                                              |

## Fee Structure

ZeeQue Preschool's fee structure is designed to be transparent and affordable, covering all essential aspects of your child's education.

### Types of Fees

| Fee Type       | Description                                                       |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Admission Fee  | One-time fee payable at the time of joining                       |
| Tuition Fee    | Payable monthly or term-wise throughout the academic year         |
| Annual Fee     | Covers academic materials, activities, and enrichment programs    |
| Transport Fee  | Optional fee based on route and distance from school              |

> **Note:** For the exact and current fee amounts, please contact the school office directly. ZeeQue offers flexible payment plans and early-bird discounts for advance registrations.

## Admission Process Steps

1. Download or collect the admission application form
2. Fill in all required details
3. Attach required documents (Aadhaar, Birth Certificate, Photos)
4. Submit form online at admission.zeeque.in or in person at campus
5. Wait for confirmation from the admissions team
6. Complete fee payment upon confirmation
7. Child begins their ZeeQue journey!
